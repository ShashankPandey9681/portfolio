I've kept notes for years — product lessons, life observations, half-baked ideas. They lived in Notion, scattered and unseen.

This site is the decision to make them public.

## Why public?

- **Accountability** — If I commit to writing here, I actually have to think clearly. No more fuzzy "I'll refine this later" drafts.
- **Connection** — The best conversations I've had started with someone saying "I read your thing and..." — I want more of those.
- **Legacy** — My kid will one day be able to read what their dad was thinking about in their first year of life.

## What you'll find here

Notes on product management, AI, productivity hacks, innovation frameworks, and the occasional honest post about life as a new dad.

No schedule. No algorithm optimization. Just writing when something's worth sharing.
