Last weekend I spent 4 hours building a CLI tool that nobody will ever use. It solves a problem only I have. And it made me a better product manager.

## The case for tinkering

When you build something end-to-end — even something tiny — you exercise muscles that atrophy in a PM role:

- **Empathy for builders** — You remember what it feels like to hit a wall, debug for an hour, and ship something imperfect.
- **Intuition for feasibility** — You develop a gut sense for "this is a weekend" vs. "this is a quarter."
- **Creative confidence** — Ideas stop feeling abstract when you can prototype them yourself.

## My rule

One side project at a time. No pressure to ship. No pressure to be useful. Just follow the curiosity.

The best PMs I know all have a tinkering habit. It keeps the craft alive.
