Nobody tells you that becoming a dad doesn't just add a responsibility — it rewrites your operating system.

## Before vs. after

Before: I optimized for output. More shipped, more written, more done.

After: I optimize for presence. Am I here — really here — for the moments that matter?

## What I've learned so far

- **Time is finite in a way that finally feels real.** Not philosophically. Viscerally.
- **Ambition doesn't die — it gets edited.** I still want to build great things. But I'm pickier about which things deserve my hours.
- **Vulnerability is strength.** Asking for help, admitting exhaustion, saying "I don't know how to do this" — that's the new default.

## For other new dads

You don't have to have it figured out. Show up. Be honest. The rest follows.
