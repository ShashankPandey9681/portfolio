I use AI every single day. Not because it's trendy — because it genuinely makes me faster at the parts of my job that don't require deep human judgment.

## Where AI shines for PMs

- **Drafting** — First drafts of PRDs, emails, and docs. I still rewrite 60% of it, but starting from something is always faster than starting from nothing.
- **Research synthesis** — Summarizing user interviews, competitor pages, and long Slack threads.
- **Data exploration** — Writing SQL queries, explaining anomalies, spotting patterns I'd miss on a Monday morning.

## Where I don't use it

- **Deciding what to build** — That requires context, empathy, and judgment no model has yet.
- **Difficult conversations** — Alignment, pushback, negotiation — that's human work.
- **Vision** — AI can extrapolate. It can't imagine what doesn't exist yet.

The line I draw: AI handles the _throughput_. I handle the _direction_.
