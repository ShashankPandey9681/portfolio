Writing in public forces clarity. When a thought has to survive contact with a
reader, the fuzzy parts show up fast. So I'm keeping this logbook: notes on
building products, working with AI, and the human side of both.

## How to publish a post

Add a Markdown file to `src/content/blog/` with this frontmatter:

- `title` — the post title
- `description` — one-line summary (shows in listings)
- `date` — publish date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- `category` — one of the six category slugs
- `draft` — optional; set `true` to hide it

That's it. Push to `main` and it's live.
