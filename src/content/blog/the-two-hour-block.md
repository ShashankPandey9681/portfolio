---

I used to start every morning in reactive mode — Slack, email, stand-up, repeat. By noon I'd feel busy but hadn't moved anything meaningful forward.

Then I tried one thing: blocking 7–9 AM as sacred thinking time. No meetings. No Slack. Just the hardest problem of the day.

## What changed

- **Better decisions** — Thinking deeply before the noise hits means I show up to meetings with clarity, not confusion.
- **Fewer meetings** — Half the "alignment" meetings I used to need disappeared because I'd already written the doc or made the call.
- **More energy for my kid** — By evening, I'm not drained from a day of shallow context-switching. I actually have presence left.

## The rule

Two hours. Every morning. Non-negotiable. Everything else can wait — and it always does.
